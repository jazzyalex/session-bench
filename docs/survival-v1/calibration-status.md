## Why this is still the intended benchmark

The product remains a bright five-angle `/100` benchmark: Record fidelity (30),
Causality and context (20), Durability and signal (15), Portability and openness (20),
and Usage and attribution (15). The 31 cells provide the research depth behind those
five quotable bars. Recovery stays an unscored diagnostic and one component of the
evidence story; it does not replace the broad ranking.

The current OpenCode result also demonstrates why v1 is deeper than v0.4: it exposes a
strong archive with precise losses instead of turning artifact presence into a perfect
score. The native record kept prompts, responses, results, causal links, revision order,
model identity, response usage, and a portable copied bundle. It did not retain every
required action field, whole-file before/after hashes, an explicit final-after-R2 chain,
or response-to-session usage reconciliation.

The remaining release-cohort work is to independently reproduce the corrected OpenCode
package and resolve its broad evidence, finish Codex CLI's current-record qualification,
close Codex Desktop's complete-root boundary, qualify one successful Claude Code CLI run,
and close Claude Desktop's cross-root and GUI-evidence contracts. Cursor research is
paused historical work; its rows remain visible in the attempt ledger, and quota failures
never become product-format failures.
