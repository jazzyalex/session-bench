# C03 archive and native-continuation contract

C03 is split into two independent qualifications:

- **C03-A archive preservation** asks whether a closed session can be reconstructed
  from a copied native bundle with the original bundle, code, application,
  backend, and network unavailable.
- **C03-B native continuation** asks whether the product can select that session,
  continue it through its native writer, preserve lineage, and avoid duplicated
  history.

An archive result may pass while continuation remains `unsupported` or
`not_run`. Such a result receives `archive_preservation_only`; it is never
silently promoted to continuation support. C06 process-termination recovery is
always recorded separately and remains incomplete until direct C06 evidence
exists. Product-specific exemptions are not supported.

The additive schema is
[`schemas/c03/v1/contract.schema.json`](../../schemas/c03/v1/contract.schema.json),
and the semantic validator is
[`session_bench/c03_contract.py`](../../session_bench/c03_contract.py). They do
not modify the accepted v1.0 prototype manifest/result schemas or historical
v0.4 files.

The sidecar binds a base manifest by path, digest, capture ID, run ID, and track.
It requires independent observation, a copied-bundle denial receipt, and exact
intact, damaged, and missing-companion controls. A passing archive must recover
the exact ordered event list without duplicates. A passing continuation must
carry independent selection and continuation observations, accepted native
continuation, and linked parent/continued-session identity.

```sh
python3 -m session_bench validate-c03 path/to/contract.json --root path/to/evidence-root
```

The validator checks the contract and its base-manifest binding. Digest fields
identify external evidence; their presence does not prove acquisition or honest
observation. A later evidence-bundle verifier must resolve and hash those
receipts. The current milestone validates the measurement contract and synthetic
controls only; it does not claim a vendor archive, continuation, or C06 result.
