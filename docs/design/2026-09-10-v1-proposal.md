# Session-Bench v1 proposal

Status: O1-O5 approved for bounded local synthetic prototype and v0.4 bookkeeping; L0/F0 live access remains separately gated. Baseline inspected at `49b7c0a`; the original design was published in `28a64aa`. This is not an implemented rubric or a measured result. v0.4 remains the published benchmark until a new implemented edition actually releases. Companion documents: [evidence and roadmap](2026-09-10-evidence-and-roadmap.md), [coverage and related work](2026-09-10-coverage-and-sources.md).

The product mission is: **Session-Bench shows which coding-agent surfaces leave a useful, independently readable record of their work—and exactly what becomes missing, ambiguous, or inaccessible.**

The technical question is: **How much of the observed work can an independent reader reconstruct from the native record?**

The public comparison/report card is the product. The atlas, native fixtures, and executable suite make it trustworthy. The report must help someone choose a configuration for an offline archive, a tool-execution audit, or recovery; no universal weighted winner does not mean no useful comparison. The central demonstration is a portable-reader challenge: reconstruct the observed work from the copied native bundle on a clean machine, without original paths, the application, or its backend, and identify every uncertainty.

Publish the atlas independently and incrementally with explicit source dates and ownership. Keep cross-agent continuation experimental because it depends on model behavior as well as record quality. Preserve v0.4 without declaring it superseded by this proposal; add a linked transition notice only when an implemented v1 edition becomes public.

## Decision summary

| Decision | Recommendation | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Product | Practical report card, supported by atlas and executable evidence | Show retained information, reader limitations, loss and uncertainty in terms developers can act on. |
| Comparison | Tested surface/configuration, linked to artifact family | Shared schemas do not prove shared writer, retention, or discovery behavior. |
| Initial platform | One CLI feasibility gate, then three contrasting macOS configurations | Prove observation/decoding before multiplying runs; include a genuine desktop path. |
| Evidence | Synthetic live-produced native records plus independent observations | Public reproduction cannot depend on private personal histories. |
| Scoring | Six protocol scenario families; separate fixed archive, portability, recovery, continuation and extended-history qualifications | Native resume and C06 do not gate an archive result; any qualification requiring C06 remains incomplete without its evidence. |
| Independence | Separate representability, writer, decoder, and reproduction claims | Prevent a decoder limitation or an unexercised operation from becoming a format failure. |
| Rollout | Measurement prototype → one-CLI end-to-end feasibility → three-configuration calibration → 36-run C01-C04 edition → recovery → conditional expansion | Success at feasibility is correct measurement of preservation and loss, not a product pass. |
| Publication | Immutable results and corrections, stable result identifiers | Research and downstream tooling must be able to reference a fixed claim. |

## Current baseline and reusable assets

The repository has ten CLI/headless harness rows, twenty scored gates, an additional untested crash gate, four computed signal gates, and sixteen other scored checklist gates. All ten current rows have missing measurements and provisional rankings. The source contains 24 test functions: 11 evaluator tests and 13 measurement tests. This design pass inspected the tests but did not execute them.

Evidence anchors:

- [README](../../README.md): current rows, missing-data caveats, standalone workflow, and historical relationship to Agent Sessions.
- [Methodology](../../methodology.md): gates, rank rules, and the one-prompt probe.
- [Evaluator](../../scripts/evaluate.py): threshold computation, checklist validation, hard-coded harness registry, generated output, and rank ranges.
- [Measurements](../../data/measurements.json): private probe provenance, mixed corpus/measurement dates, and missing physical SQLite probe measurement.
- [Evidence limits](../../evidence/README.md): underlying raw artifacts and corpus transcripts are not public.
- [S4 receipt](../../evidence/receipts-2026-08-22-s4.md): contributed measurements without artifact hashes; filtered-row denominator and byte-count caveats are disclosed.
- [Backlog](../../BACKLOG.md): useful harness/surface/artifact distinction, paired probes, local-only boundary, and companion-store requirements.

Preserve deterministic generation, input validation, exact-fraction comparisons for the historical edition, receipt-query tests, extraction byte accounting, correction history, and provenance metadata. Reuse implementation only after testing its specific contract. Do not transplant the current rank formula or content-key heuristic into the new primary metrics.

The main limitation is observation reproducibility, not arithmetic reproducibility. Existing unit tests cannot establish that a live product preserved its work. The generic JSONL extractor silently skips malformed records in semantic counts, while retaining them in its line count; v1 needs explicit diagnostics. Existing SQLite measurements need a defined capture/snapshot contract before physical footprint and live consistency claims. The comment that read-only SQLite cannot see WAL rows conflicts with the adjacent WAL test; v1 must define supported capture behavior rather than inherit that comment.

The present rubric also embeds architectural preferences. Single-file tailing, no sidecars, plaintext containers, embedded dollar figures, and naive linear replay should become descriptive properties or outcome-based tests. Stable joins can be good; a documented binary decoder can be usable; a migration can improve storage while preserving old records. Rationale visibility is descriptive, not proof of actual cognition.

## Scope and identity

Native local persistence is the primary track. Explicit vendor exports are a separate track with acquisition steps, authentication requirements, omissions, and export version recorded. Network telemetry can supply independent observations when authorized, but cannot silently replace missing native records. Cloud-only products may receive atlas entries and an explicit local-availability result; they do not qualify as tested native-local implementations through an export.

Use separate identities rather than one overloaded result/run tuple:

- `run_id`: exact live distribution/build, surface/launch mode, OS/build, model/provider, configuration class and settings, workload/helper and observer versions, execution/attempt history.
- `capture_id`: live-run reference, acquisition adapter/procedure, capture boundary/type, native artifact inventory and digests. One live run may have pre- and post-recovery captures.
- `evaluation_id`: capture digest, decoder/comparator/evaluator versions, protocol/scenario/assertion-set versions, observation/expectation digests, output digests. A decoder update produces a new evaluation, not a new live run or a vendor retention improvement.

Record format/schema identity separately. Verification is a set of dated attestations bound to these IDs: submission, maintainer inspection, independent recomputation, and independent live reproduction. They coexist; a single apparent trust ladder must not overwrite one achievement with another.

The default-configuration baseline is mandatory for evaluated configurations. Start from a clean supported profile with ordinary persistence settings and document defaults, model selection and unavoidable account/workspace setup. Enhanced retention, verbose diagnostics, changed privacy settings or alternative launch modes get separate result identities. Observation instrumentation must disclose any behavior it changes; if it changes native persistence, that run cannot stand in for the ordinary baseline. Interactive, one-shot/headless, desktop-local and remote modes never substitute silently for one another.

Distinguish the writer's application version from a format/schema version. A missing schema version remains `unknown`; it does not erase the known writer version. Record launch evidence and the exact executable/app identity. Do not infer the originating surface from a shared directory.

The atlas links each surface to one or more artifact families. A manifest lists root roles, physical encoding/tables, session identifiers, joins, attachments, subagent stores, metadata, retention, and acquisition method. A diagnostic log becomes part of the required bundle if it contains unique in-scope session information; classification follows function, not filename. Unrelated caches and credentials remain outside collection. Local-only scope does not grant access to existing personal stores.

Reuse format documentation and decoder fixtures when schemas genuinely match. Retain separate live-writer evidence for each surface and mode. If identical content is stored in multiple locations, keep the physical artifacts and evaluate logical deduplication explicitly.

## Claims and states

Every assertion declares one of four subjects:

| Subject | Permitted claim | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Representability | Format can encode a feature | Public schema/source or labeled constructed fixture; not proof the product writes it. |
| Writer behavior | Tested build preserved a particular observed event | Native live-produced bundle plus independent observation of that event. |
| Decoder correctness | This decoder reconstructs expected content/relationships | Public fixtures, precise expected outputs, and source-record locators. |
| Reproduction | Another execution or implementation agrees | Separate attestation identifying what was repeated and by whom. |

Use separate axes rather than one overloaded verdict:

- Execution: `valid`, `not_run`, `invalid`, `not_exercised`. Include a reason code and all attempts.
- Applicability: `required`, `optional_supported`, `unsupported`, `unknown`. Unsupported requires evidence; failed access is not unsupported.
- Assertion: `pass`, `fail`, `unresolved`. A failure names its subject; uncertainty about decoder versus writer is unresolved at writer level.
- Evidence: multiple attestations of kinds `submission`, `maintainer_inspection`, `independent_recomputation`, and `independent_live_reproduction`, each with subject IDs, actor, date, scope and limitations. None overwrites the others.

A feature absent from a native artifact does not automatically establish writer failure: first prove the event happened, check the full declared bundle, and independently validate the decoder interpretation. Conversely, product data loss after a valid trigger remains a failure even if the product later errors. An infrastructure/auth failure before exercising the trigger is invalid or not run. Retain all such attempts, not only successes.

Keep six scenario families in the protocol, but remove the all-purpose `Core conformant` designation. Each named qualification has a fixed mandatory assertion set frozen before scored collection. Unsupported, missing or unresolved required assertions leave that qualification incomplete; they never shrink its denominator. A failing but verified result is publishable. Always show its diagnostic counts and successful assertions. Unsupported classification needs pinned product/version capability evidence and documented trigger inspection; search silence, access failure or model refusal is insufficient.

Every assertion's result card distinguishes:

| Public outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Retained and reconstructed | Native evidence contains the fact; this decoder reconstructs the scored fields correctly. |
| Retained, decoder incomplete | Independent inspection locates the fact, but the decoder omits or misreads it. |
| Verified absent | The event was independently observed at the scored boundary; the complete declared bundle was inspected and the absence established within that scope. |
| Unresolved | Acquisition, source coverage, presentation, or interpretation cannot distinguish retention from loss. |

Wrong retained values, such as a success status for an independently observed failure, receive a separate explicit `contradicted` finding on the affected fields and fail the assertion. Do not relabel a wrong value as recovered because its ID exists. Result packets identify observation, native locators, mismatch, affected profile and minimal reproduction steps. Source coverage gaps prevent a claim of universal absence.

## Public qualifications

| Qualification | Fixed mandatory assertions | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Readable archive | C01 accepted content/roles/order; C02 coding action trail, scored arguments/outcomes and relationships; C03-A preservation after normal close | Native continuation, cancellation, termination recovery, or complete historical working-tree backup. |
| Portable bundle | All Readable archive assertions plus C04 enumeration, attribution, dependency closure and copied offline reconstruction | Recovery after process termination or a vendor export being equivalent to native storage. |
| Lifecycle recovery | C05 completed-prefix and incomplete-state assertions plus C06-A/B/C settled-prefix and truthful-state assertions, all required repetitions | Power-loss safety or success on untested conditions. |
| Session continuation | C03-A archive preservation and C03-B correct target selection, accepted continuation and lineage | Retention of failed tools elsewhere, lifecycle recovery, or model recall accuracy. |
| Extended agent history | Separate fixed subprofile sets for exercised branches/subagents, compaction and attachments | Passing one subprofile does not qualify another or imply all extended history is covered. |

All prerequisites and repetitions are versioned. No product-specific C06 exemptions: inability to isolate persistence is a measurement limitation, never a passing recovery result. Archive/portable results can stand independently, but any designation requiring C06 stays incomplete until valid evidence exists. The first C01-C04 edition reports only the qualifications it actually establishes and visibly marks recovery untested.

Openness remains a distinct, useful report-card dimension: public documentation, decoder availability/license and pinned usable version, obtainable dependencies, and requirements for the original application/authentication/backend. A successful private reverse-engineered reader proves neither open tooling nor a healthy ecosystem. Report these properties without rewarding plaintext containers by default.

## Independent observation and normalization

Use a deterministic local fixture project with innocuous files, unique per-run markers, a controlled tool helper, and externally recorded interactions. Expected data is created before decoding and is never inferred from the native output being evaluated.

Enforce input separation, not just separate modules:

```text
Decoder:   native-only package + format adapter → reconstructed records + native locators
Evaluator: reconstructed records + independent observations + frozen assertions → outcomes
```

The decoder has no observer transcript, workload answers, expected outcomes, private original roots or network access. Its package contains native artifacts and only the acquisition metadata required to identify/decode them, never expected facts. The evaluation workspace is separate. Qualification tests change the observer/answer key while holding native input fixed and require identical decoded output. Removing a scored fact from every native source must prevent recovery of that fact even if it remains in the observer ledger.

Before vendor scoring, positive and derived negative fixtures must detect omitted responses/actions, altered exit status, duplicate events, unresolved companions and absent bundles. Distinguish an empty native bundle captured correctly (a potentially valid absence outcome) from an evidence bundle missing from delivery (invalid evidence). Known corrupt native data is not automatically invalid capture.

The observer records submitted/accepted user interactions, controlled helper invocations/results, file changes, and lifecycle triggers. Each observation has its own ID, origin, monotonic sequence, timestamp precision, and acquisition evidence. Desktop actions require actual UI execution evidence; launching a CLI in a desktop terminal does not test the desktop surface. Record human interventions and do not silently replace difficult UI steps with headless equivalents.

Permitted assistant-content observations are a captured live stdout/PTY display stream for the exact CLI mode, or accessibility text accompanied by UI screenshots/recording for desktop. Reading saved history, a transcript export, or a history endpoint that loads the same stored artifact is not independent truth. An independently tapped live response stream may supplement observation only when authorized, with its relationship to displayed content documented. Core C01 evaluates final visible assistant message blocks, including refusals and visible errors, with `completed`, `interrupted`, or `error` status; intermediate token deltas belong to an optional streaming profile. If a turn has several visible blocks, all independently captured blocks enter the population. No assistant output is a recorded fact, not an invented empty response. OCR-only extraction without reviewed uncertainty cannot support exact-text claims. Observer and decoder must be separate modules; disclose shared code, identifiers, and writer-supplied metadata so common failure sources are visible.

Measure only what the observer can establish. A helper invocation proves that helper action, not every uninstrumented built-in tool or hidden internal model request. Record visibility by event class. When a surface cannot supply independent observation of assistant content, relevant completeness assertions remain unresolved; do not infer omitted responses from the same transcript. Model IDs and token quantities need an independent source before accuracy claims; otherwise test availability and internal consistency only.

Compare corresponding boundaries: `helper_invoked`, `helper_emitted`, `harness_received`, and `displayed` are different observations. Helper output emission alone cannot prove delivery to the harness. A native shell-tool envelope may legitimately contain several helper subprocesses; one action may also span several native records. Use explicit one-to-many/many-to-one mappings with source locators and observed command/process ancestry where available; never equate helper count with native-tool-call count. If association or receipt cannot be established, report that field unresolved. Do not call a missing terminal-subprocess exit status a writer omission when only an enclosing shell exit was independently observed.

The normalized representation is a comparison view, not a required vendor format. It contains sessions, messages, tool attempts/results, lifecycle boundaries, relationships, usage observations, attachments, and explicit unknowns. Every item maps back to native evidence. Distinguish `absent`, `unknown`, `redacted`, `truncated`, and `not_observed`; never coerce them into empty text or zero.

Preserve native timestamps separately from observer timestamps. Use partial-order constraints for concurrent work; do not invent a total order or equate file order with causality. Preserve active/abandoned branches, summaries, and original records separately. Unknown native event types remain accounted for and visible in coverage diagnostics.

## Field-specific recovery contract

An event is fully recovered only if all fields required for its assertion match. Publish identification, arguments, result, status and relationships independently as well as an all-required-fields count. Matching an ID alone is insufficient. Zero observed events is unresolved, not 100% recovery.

| Field | Deterministic comparison rule |
|---|---|
| Logical identity | Preserve native IDs where present; permit decoder-derived locators/occurrence IDs with `identity_origin`. Two intentionally identical messages remain distinct by occurrence and causal/turn position, not content deduplication. |
| Visible conversation | Compare final visible blocks after a pinned presentation transform appropriate to the observed surface. Strip only proven terminal control/presentation effects; keep raw PTY/AX evidence and transform version. |
| Markdown and code | Raw Markdown equality only when both observations contain raw Markdown. Rendered-text comparison must use a pinned render transform; unknown rendering is unresolved. Preserve code-block indentation, internal whitespace and explicit newlines; do not globally trim or collapse whitespace. |
| Terminal layout | Reconstruct terminal redraws at captured dimensions using a pinned terminal-state parser. Remove soft-wrap artifacts only when the capture identifies them; ambiguous wraps/hidden blocks yield presentation uncertainty. |
| Arguments/results | Exact bytes at a common observed boundary, or a field-specific declared equivalence such as JSON object key order. JSON arrays, strings, missing/null values, exit codes and error classes remain significant. |
| File action trail | Match action kind and project-relative target plus independently observed outcome and relationship. File digests/diffs serve as observer evidence; reconstructing every historical file byte is a separate optional claim. |
| Time and relationships | Test declared precision and partial-order edges. Do not invent sub-millisecond accuracy, total ordering or parentage from ambiguous timestamps. |

No LLM semantic-equivalence grading. Where deterministic presentation comparison is infeasible, localize the unresolved fields; do not let an approximate clean transcript pass exact-content assertions. Canonicalization must not use expected text to repair decoded text.

## Scenario specifications

These are proposed contracts, not executed tests. The first evaluated edition runs C01-C04 three times on each of three configurations: 36 scheduled scenario runs. C03-A/B are assertion groups within one C03 run, not extra runs. Each C04 scenario run creates two native sessions; that multiplicity is recorded per capture and is not converted into a total here. Later C05 adds nine scenario runs and C06 adds 27, bringing the same configurations to 72 scheduled scenario runs. Gates, retries and advanced-profile calibration are additional; their native-session counts are not inferred before collection. Later rows cannot reuse runs from another surface or protocol version.

| ID | Trigger and independent truth | Required assertions | Invalid/unexercised boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| C01 Conversation | Submit three short turns including a correction; capture accepted text and visible completed blocks independently. | Accepted content, scored rendered/raw response fields, roles, turn order and distinguishable occurrences reconstruct without duplicates. Preserve correction as history. | Prompt not accepted or observer missed a response: affected assertions unexercised/unresolved; never demand a particular generated answer or native ID format. |
| C02 Coding actions and outcomes | Inspect a small deterministic project, run a known failing test, make a small file edit, then correct/retry and rerun the deterministic test. Independently observe actual invocations, file changes and test outcomes. | Reconstruct the inspected/changed target, actual attempted actions, scored arguments, failure and later outcome, and links between actions/results. Match subprocess observations to native envelopes at declared boundaries. | Skipped operation = not exercised for that assertion; wrong solution = task outcome. Actual performed failed attempts remain scored; observer failure invalidates only unsupported claims. |
| C03-A Archive after close; C03-B Native continuation | A: capture completed work, close normally, reconstruct archived history independently. B: when supported, freeze target locator before close, enumerate after reopen, select it and send a continuation. | A: completed observed history survives without requiring product resume. B: intended session is selectable, continuation accepted with correct lineage, no duplicate logical history. | Unsupported B leaves Session continuation incomplete but does not prevent A/archive qualification. Operator error invalid only for an unambiguous supplied locator; product ambiguity is measured. No model-recall requirement. |
| C04 Discovery and copied bundle | Create two sessions in the fixture project; capture root changes; copy declared bundle into an offline reader workspace. | Enumerate both, attribute fixture project, resolve required joins, reconstruct prior observed content without vendor backend/UI or accidental access to original paths. | Capture cannot establish coherent snapshot = invalid. No durable local record is an observed limitation, not a successful empty bundle. |
| C05 Cancellation | Start a controlled long operation, observe its start, cancel through the tested surface, then continue if supported. | Previously completed work remains; pending work is not falsely marked completed; cancellation/incompleteness is explicit; later work is linked correctly. | Operation completed before trigger = not exercised; UI trigger unconfirmed = invalid. No assertion that cancellation undoes filesystem side effects. |
| C06 Process-termination recovery | Observe completed markers; terminate the declared persistence process set at a quiet-period barrier, tool-start barrier, or tool-result barrier; freeze post-termination evidence before reopening. | Require recovery of the settled completed prefix and truthful incomplete-state handling. Separately report immediate recent-event loss and any explicitly acknowledged-persistence loss. | Wrong process killed or observer/capture failed = invalid. Data loss after a valid trigger remains an outcome. No inference of vendor flush acknowledgement from visible completion. |

C02's fixture should contain a small known defect with a deterministic regression test. Require an initial test attempt to expose the failure, an actual edit, then another test attempt. Observe before/after synthetic file snapshots and test-run exit/output independently. If the agent fixes the code before exercising the failing test, retain that outcome and mark the missing failure branch unexercised rather than inventing it or penalizing the format. A final failing test can still be faithfully archived. The benchmark checks the action trail, not coding success or reconstruction of every historical source-file byte. An operation's raw arguments/outcome are mandatory only at explicitly scored observable boundaries; missing instrumentation must leave the required assertion unresolved, never quietly remove it from the profile.

For C06 use three separately reported conditions, each repeated three times: (A) after a fixed quiet interval following the final visible response, (B) after a new controlled helper starts, and (C) after that helper emits its result. All conditions begin with the same independently observed prefix followed by the fixed quiet interval. The proposed interval is 2 seconds, subject to public calibration and freezing before scored collection; it is a benchmark user-outcome threshold, not a vendor durability promise. No per-product tuning. Recovery assertions concern that settled prefix and absence of fabricated completion; recent events at B/C yield diagnostic loss-window measurements with their exact observed boundary, not unsupported delivery/flush claims. Independently observable persistence acknowledgements support an additional assertion; missing acknowledgement is unknown and does not improve a result.

Predeclare executable paths/builds and the persistence process tree or daemon set, termination mechanism/signal, supervisor relationship, auto-relaunch controls, and post-kill liveness check. Kill only isolated benchmark processes. If the persistence process cannot be identified or recovery cannot be stopped before capture, the condition is invalid. Use a helper/controller handshake for B/C, record monotonic trigger and termination times, and publish the observed timing window. Events whose relative order is ambiguous within that window are unresolved for immediate-loss metrics. Capture the full declared artifact set before recovery, including present WAL/journal companions. This measures controlled process-termination recovery, not power-loss durability or a probability of safety under arbitrary crashes.

Capture validity and native-data health are independent. A coherent logical snapshot may support ordinary reconstruction; a frozen physical post-termination capture preserves corruption and WAL/journal state for recovery. A logical backup cannot replace the latter or establish physical footprint. Correctly captured corruption is a valid product outcome, even when integrity checks fail; capture-invalid means acquisition failed to establish what was collected. SQLite readers can consult committed WAL data, and the backup API serves a different snapshot purpose. [WAL documentation](https://sqlite.org/wal.html), [Backup API](https://sqlite.org/backup.html).

## Extended and descriptive profiles

| Profile | Scenario and assertions |
|---|---|
| Branches and collaboration | Fork/rewind, two concurrent helpers, two subagents, and side thread. Match independently triggered identities; reconstruct parent/child and active branch; preserve abandoned paths without merging them. No inference of links from titles alone when testing explicit attribution. |
| Compaction | Trigger supported compaction after known markers. Test archival preservation separately from effective resumed context. Identify summary boundary, lineage, and documented omissions; do not demand preservation of hidden model state. |
| Attachments and large outputs | Supply a synthetic image/file and controlled large output. Check payload or stable resolvable reference, content hashes, truncation markers, and dependencies. Do not demand identical image encoding. |
| Orphan resilience | Derive fixtures by removing one declared companion/attachment at a time. Expected result is bounded partial recovery plus explicit missing dependency; never call this a live writer test. |
| Compatibility | Decode public prior-build fixtures with current decoder; separately perform authorized writer upgrade and test original/copy histories. Relocate fixture workspace and distinguish discovery changes from content loss. Publish a version-pair matrix. |
| Retention and redaction | Plant only synthetic markers in declared public-content and sensitive-input channels. Compare native retention and explicit export policy separately. A marker intentionally requested as transcript content is not a leak by definition. Publish policy-conformance results, not a universal 'nothing retained' score. |
| Usage and provenance | Attribute messages/model changes, requests, cache units, retries, and aggregation boundaries. Test availability and reconciliation; distinguish provider-reported, estimated, billed, and subscription units. |
| Incremental reading | Repeated observation while writer runs. Measure visibility delay, duplicates, omitted completed events, and consistent snapshots. Polling a database is allowed; a single tail-able file is not required. |
| Efficiency | Report physical bundle bytes, logical payload bytes, cold-start and marginal growth, attachments, WAL, and semantic duplication with precise denominators. Evaluate both full event-history and final-state views; state information lost by every collapse rule. |
| Export and handoff | Evaluate explicit exports against the native bundle as a separate track. Later test cross-agent continuation with fixed tasks and separate model outcomes; it is not an archive qualification requirement. |

Calibrate at least one branch, subagent, compaction or attachment profile early, before the first evaluated edition, with a separately declared additional run/time allowance. Select a concrete supported trigger during calibration preflight. If it cannot be exercised, report the limitation and do not claim broad agent-history coverage. Each extended subprofile fixes its assertions/repetitions before qualification; one exploratory calibration attempt is method evidence, not a profile pass.

Privacy has competing useful outcomes: faithful local retention and safe intentional sharing. Neither should be judged solely by how much text is stored. If artifact sanitization changes a scored property, invalidate that measurement or regenerate the synthetic run; do not publish a flattering transformed artifact as raw evidence.

## Metrics, repetition, and reporting

Publish counts and per-run outcomes before rates. Full recovery is events whose required identification/content/status/relationship assertions all match divided by independently observed in-scope expected events. Publish field-level rates separately; an ID match is not full recovery. A zero denominator is unresolved, not 100%. Subprocess-action populations and native tool-envelope populations stay separate. Report duplicates, tool/result association, attribution, order constraints, unresolved joins, missing dependencies and recovery loss with explicit units and denominators.

No current arbitrary byte threshold carries over. Efficiency curves share the same semantic workload and distinguish cold/warm storage. A small incomplete record cannot outrank a faithful record through storage size. Sampling, truncation, and privacy mode are part of configuration identity.

Evaluated C01-C05 families use three repetitions with fresh run IDs; C06 uses three repetitions per condition in the separate recovery stage. A scheduled C03 run without native continuation can still provide C03-A archive evidence while C03-B remains unsupported/unexercised. Report conditions separately, all outcomes, range, median where meaningful, and exact sample size. Three successes do not establish a long-run failure rate. Confirmation campaigns may increase repetitions under a declared budget while preserving initial failures. Do not treat within-session events as independent runs for confidence intervals.

Predeclare one retry allowance for infrastructure failures per scheduled run; preserve the failed attempt and stop on recurring auth/runtime failure. Do not retry valid failures to obtain green rows. Changed prompts, permissions, models, or persistence settings create a new configuration result. Optional features are triggered intentionally; omission by the model is not evidence of format absence.

The public report answers: which instructions/corrections were accepted, which actions were attempted or failed, what changed, what remained incomplete, which session/branch owns each fact, and what is unavailable. Deterministic assertion results support these answers; no narrative grading is needed. Show default/enhanced configuration, qualification, retained-versus-decoded outcome, native evidence and attestations on the card. Never average an untested desktop surface into a CLI score. Decoder changes create new evaluations of old captures, not claims of vendor improvement. Drift flags trigger review; neither a field rename nor a stable schema alone determines compatibility.

The atlas has its own documentation release gate and maintenance owner, separate inspection/test dates and stale-source handling. Measured report cards add per-result dispute/correction handling and immutable supersession links. It can ship without conformance results; no first-edition qualification is implied by a documentation-only page.

## Feasibility, first edition, and expansion

1. The approved and accepted first implementation scope is the bounded measurement prototype: native-only decoder, observer/evaluator boundary, deterministic comparisons and positive/negative fixtures. The complete expansion access plan is not a prerequisite to that authorization.
2. Before three-configuration calibration, prove C01+C02 end to end on one named default CLI configuration, proposed Codex interactive local. Obtain at least one valid run of each family, copy the captured native bundle, decode/evaluate offline with original roots inaccessible, and evaluate a derived fixture with a scored fact removed or damaged. Gate success means the measurement distinguishes preservation from loss, including a legitimate failing product result. It does not mean the product passes. Actual live use still requires a bounded authorized access/usage envelope for this CLI.
3. Calibrate three contrasting configurations, proposed Codex interactive CLI, Claude Desktop Code/Local, and Goose interactive CLI. Confirm their actual storage and observer differences; nominal product diversity alone is not proof. Include one early advanced-profile trigger with a separately budgeted attempt. No silent CLI substitute for desktop.
4. Freeze C01-C04 and run three repetitions on each configuration: 36 scheduled scenario runs. Each C04 run creates two native sessions. Release only evidenced archive/portable/continuation results, with recovery explicitly untested and independent copied-bundle reproduction demonstrated. Unexercised required assertions remain incomplete; valid failures remain publishable.
5. Qualify recovery separately on the same configurations: C05 three repetitions and C06 three conditions × three repetitions. This adds 36 scheduled scenario runs, 72 total across evaluated stages before gates/retries/profiles. C06 isolation difficulty never earns an exemption or pass; native-session totals are established from actual captures.
6. Broaden only with a concrete campaign plan naming surfaces and launch modes, verified access, and explicit spend, quota-consumption and operator-time caps. Stop at caps; never hide unavailable rows. The former eight-surface/192-run plan is an expansion sizing example, not the first release commitment or current authorization.

## Alternatives and tradeoffs

| Alternative | Benefit | Cost or risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broader v0.4 leaderboard only | Fastest visible expansion | Retains weak evidence and architectural bias | Preserve historically, do not make it the new standard. |
| Atlas only | Low execution cost, immediately useful | Documentation does not establish persistence behavior | Ship independently; keep practical measured report cards as the overall product. |
| One universal weighted score | Simple headline | Weights hide loss and incentivize denominator gaming | Defer; use profiles and explicit outcomes. |
| Mandatory common vendor schema | Simpler downstream parsing | Adoption burden; erases native distinctions | Use an internal comparison model with native locators. |
| Full task-performance benchmark | Familiar success metric | Duplicates other work and confounds storage with model capability | Keep task success separate from record qualifications. |
| Every surface at launch | Broad marketing claim | Repeats unqualified methodology across many configurations | One CLI feasibility, three-configuration first edition, conditional expansion. |
| Private real-history corpus first | Realistic behavior | Publication and independent reproduction blocked | Use synthetic live-generated evidence first; field corpus is separate. |
